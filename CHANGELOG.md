# Changelog

## Unreleased

 * THE JOURNEY CARD on the case file replaces the step bar and the separate row of moves: one card, *The journey · step n of 5 · <place>*, above the index, its five places stacked on a line. A reached place says when and by whom; the current place carries the legal move as the house `.cta` at the right end of its row; a place ahead says what reaches it ("when the claimant is paid, or the money is waived", "by itself, 30 days after resolved", with the date once resolved). A row takes the house row tint on hover, the current row a stronger one at rest. The dashboard's "where things stand" widget keeps the rail (`.i-wf`, `.i-trans`).
 * INCIDENTS AT ORGANIZATION SCOPE: three screens across every area the module is switched on in, mounted by the shell from the provider's `orgPages()` (tagged `shell.org_pages`) — the Observatory row, the tab strip *Overview · Incidents · Incident kinds* and the scope control are the shell's. `/incidents` is the Overview: the strip (areas, filed this month, in progress, critical open) and open work past its term as the shell's banner. `/incidents/list` is every area's register in one house table, the area as a column, under the house bar — area, category, status, month and department chips and the search — and the shell's foot. `/incidents/kinds` is the one taxonomy down the left and, for the chosen word, each area's figures with a door to that area's kinds editor, under the bar and over the foot. Every figure is the area pages' own reading added (`IncidentOrgService`); an area that has not switched incidents on, or whose register the reader may not open, is not read. The organization dashboard's incidents card opens `/incidents/list` when the open work spans areas. The module's icon is `incident:triangle-alert`, shipped by the module, so the sidebar can draw it.
 * The suite sets `memory_limit` to `-1` in `phpunit.dist.xml`, so a full run does not inherit a developer's ini.

 * THE CASE FILE IS AN INDEX OVER ONE COLUMN: the facts strip and the rail stay at the top, and the shell's in-page index (`@Shell/_onpage.html.twig`) runs beside one column of full-width cards, one row per card — Where, Narrative, Timeline, Evidence, Money, Involved parties, Provenance & links, then under the head *The steps* a row per step reached and *Category fields*. A card the reader may not see (the case file, the money) has no row either. The steps are one column too, and the Where map is the house record plate, `min(58vh,560px)`. The fields heading reads "What this category asks for".

# Changelog

## Unreleased

 * A REPORT THAT COMES BACK KEEPS EVERYTHING IT WAS POSTED WITH — the line, the point, when, how bad, their own words, the sub-category and every block's answers, each repeated row drawn again (`Model/ReportAnswers`). Any refusal: the point outside the area, or a missing answer.
 * THE BANNER IS THERE AS SOON AS THE POINT IS OUTSIDE: the form asks `GET /areas/{uuid}/modules/incidents/placement?lat=&lng=` (`incident_placement`, behind `incidents.record`) whenever the point changes. The JSON says inside or outside, the metres beyond, the area's setting, its name and the zone, and carries the banner and the Where note drawn by the same partials the form renders (`report/_placement`, `report/_where`). The Where row reads "outside <area> · no zone", or the zone a point inside is in; under Refuse File stays shut with "a point inside <area>" owed. A form arriving with a point is judged on its first paint. The server judges the point again on filing.
 * AN INCIDENT FILED OUTSIDE ITS AREA SAYS SO on its record: "filed outside <area>" under the Zone fact and on the Where card's tab.

 * A REPORT PLACED OUTSIDE ITS AREA'S BOUNDARY (Settings › Modules › Incidents › Placement, per area): Accept, Warn (the default) or Refuse, and "Inside if within" (500 m, 0–10000). Beyond the boundary by more than that, the web form under Warn holds the report with a banner — "Move the point" / "File it outside the area", the second posting `placement=outside` — and under Refuse offers only Move the point and keeps "a point inside <area>" owed until the point moves. Every filing is judged and marked (`incident.outside_area`, migration `Version20261108120000`); a report from a phone is always filed and marked. Needs core 0.3's choice meanings, setting notes and the banner's submit-button door.

 * No sheet the module ships gives a house fold a transition of its own (`tests/Unit/Template/FoldConformanceTest`).

 * THE MODULE RUNS ON CORE 0.3 (`uhifadhi/uhifadhi` `^0.3`), the line whose Atlas draws every Chart.js kind: the charts name their kind with the Contracts enum, `Uhifadhi\Contracts\Performance\ChartKind`, since `Uhifadhi\Bundle\AtlasBundle\Model\ChartKind` is gone from the core.

 * THE JOURNEY CARD on the case file replaces the step bar and the separate row of moves: one card, *The journey · step n of 5 · <place>*, above the index, its five places stacked on a line. A reached place says when and by whom; the current place carries the legal move as the house `.cta` at the right end of its row; a place ahead says what reaches it ("when the claimant is paid, or the money is waived", "by itself, 30 days after resolved", with the date once resolved). A row takes the house row tint on hover, the current row a stronger one at rest. The dashboard's "where things stand" widget keeps the rail (`.i-wf`, `.i-trans`).
 * INCIDENTS AT ORGANIZATION SCOPE: two screens across every area the module is switched on in, mounted by the shell from the provider's `orgPages()` (tagged `shell.org_pages`) — the Observatory row, the tab strip *Incidents · Incident kinds* and the scope control are the shell's. `/incidents`, where the module lands, is every area's register in one house table, the area as a column, under the house bar — area, category, status, month and department chips and the search — and the shell's foot. `/incidents/kinds` is the one taxonomy down the left and, for the chosen word, each area's figures with a door to that area's kinds editor, under the bar and over the foot. Every figure is the area pages' own reading added (`IncidentOrgService`); an area that has not switched incidents on, or whose register the reader may not open, is not read. The organization dashboard's incidents card opens `/incidents` when the open work spans areas. The module's icon is `incident:triangle-alert`, shipped by the module, so the sidebar can draw it.
 * The suite sets `memory_limit` to `-1` in `phpunit.dist.xml`, so a full run does not inherit a developer's ini.

 * THE CASE FILE IS AN INDEX OVER ONE COLUMN: the facts strip and the rail stay at the top, and the shell's in-page index (`@Shell/_onpage.html.twig`) runs beside one column of full-width cards, one row per card — Where, Narrative, Timeline, Evidence, Money, Involved parties, Provenance & links, then under the head *The steps* a row per step reached and *Category fields*. A card the reader may not see (the case file, the money) has no row either. The steps are one column too, and the Where map is the house record plate, `min(58vh,560px)`. The fields heading reads "What this category asks for".
